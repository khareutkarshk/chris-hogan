The OVER / UNDER Framework™
A Practical Process for Regaining Control, Reclaiming Value, and Rebuilding Momentum

Most people do not have a capability problem.

They have an overload problem.

They are:

Overthinking
Overstressed
Overworked
Overwhelmed

And as a result, they often feel:

Underappreciated
Undervalued
Underpaid
Undersexed

The solution is not simply "work harder."

The solution is learning how to manage the OVERS and strategically improve the UNDERS.

PART I: The OVER Method™
Step 1: OVERTHINKING
The THINK → DECIDE → ACT Rule

Overthinkers often confuse thinking with progress.

The brain creates three piles:

Things I can control.
Things I can influence.
Things I cannot control.

Most anxiety lives in pile #3.

The Process

Ask:

What is the actual problem?

Then answer:

What do I know?
What am I assuming?
What action can I take today?
The 10-10-10 Test

Will this matter:

10 minutes from now?
10 months from now?
10 years from now?

Most worries fail this test.

Improvement Framework

Stop asking:

"What if?"

Start asking:

"What now?"

Action kills anxiety faster than analysis.

Step 2: OVERSTRESSED
The PRESSURE Audit™

Stress occurs when demands exceed resources.

The solution isn't eliminating pressure.

The solution is increasing capacity.

Identify Your Four Tanks
Physical Tank
Sleep
Nutrition
Water
Exercise
Mental Tank
Learning
Reading
Reflection
Emotional Tank
Relationships
Gratitude
Laughter
Spiritual Tank
Purpose
Faith
Values
The Rule

Never make major life decisions when all four tanks are empty.

Improvement Framework

Each morning ask:

"What fills me?"

Each evening ask:

"What drained me?"

Do more of one.
Reduce the other.

Step 3: OVERWORKED
The Productivity Truth

Most people aren't overworked.

They're under-prioritized.

The 3D Method™

Every task falls into one category:

DO

Only things you must personally do.

DELEGATE

Things others can do.

DELETE

Things that don't matter.

Weekly Question

"If I stopped doing this for 90 days, would anyone notice?"

If not:

Delete it.

Improvement Framework

Replace busy with productive.

Activity is not achievement.

Movement is not progress.

Step 4: OVERWHELMED
The Mountain Method™

Overwhelm happens when your brain tries carrying the entire mountain at once.

Process

Write everything down.

Everything.

No filtering.

No organizing.

No judging.

Then categorize:

Now

Must happen today.

Next

Needs attention this week.

Later

Can wait.

Never

Should disappear.

Improvement Framework

Your life is not a mountain.

It is simply the next step.

Focus on one step.

Then another.

Then another.

Impact lasts longer.
